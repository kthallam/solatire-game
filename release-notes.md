First Android preview of Quiet Spider, an original offline Spider Solitaire game.

- No advertising, accounts, analytics, or internet permission.
- 1-, 2-, and 4-suit games, hints, undo, and automatic progress saving.
- Android 6.0 or later.

Download quiet-spider-v0.1.0-debug.apk below and open it on your Android phone. Android may ask you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.

This is a debug-signed preview, not a Play Store release. The APK build and 4,817 rules checks passed; Android lint completed with one backup-settings warning. Device/emulator playtesting has not been performed.
